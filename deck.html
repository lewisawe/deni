The problem

8M+

Kenyans are blacklisted by credit bureaus, most over mobile loans, many under KES 200. A listing locks people out of jobs, rent, and further credit.

Two facts are hidden at the moment you decide.

The cost: "repay 1,150 on 1,000 in 30 days" looks like 15%. It is 182.5% APR.

The right: Is the lender licensed? Can they legally chase you? What can they take? Almost no borrower knows to ask.
