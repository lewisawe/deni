# Deni: Demo Video Script

> Target length: 2:30–3:00. Format: mp4/mov/webm/avi, ≤250MB.
> Record a backup take end to end before the live day, in case Bedrock is throttled
> (the deterministic core still works offline; see the fallback notes).
> Cross-track: Transparency & Accountability (primary) + Safety, Reporting & Protection
> + Stability & Social Cohesion.

## Before you record

- Log in / warm up AWS Bedrock so the AI parse and explanation are responsive. If it is
  throttled, the cost engine, licence check, rights, recourse (keyword fallback),
  redaction, USSD and WhatsApp all still work. Nothing on screen breaks.
- Set the language to English for the main run; do one toggle to Kiswahili/Sheng on
  camera to prove the multilingual claim.
- Zoom the browser so the APR number and the value-vs-pay bar are large on screen.
- Have a second phone or a WhatsApp/USSD simulator window ready for the channels beat.

## The one-sentence hook (say this first, over the landing hero)

"Millions of Kenyans are locked out of the economy over small mobile loans they never
understood. Deni shows a borrower the real cost, whether the lender is even legal, and
exactly what to do about it, on any phone, in their language."

## Shot list (build backward from the tracks)

### 0:00–0:20 — The problem, on the landing page
- Open `/` (landing). The hero shows the value proposition and a live true-cost card.
- Voiceover: "A loan that says 'borrow 1,000, repay 1,150 in 30 days' looks like 15%.
  It is 182.5% a year. Deni makes that visible."
- Scroll once to the "8,000,000+ blacklisted" number. Keep it to one beat.

### 0:20–0:45 — True cost (Transparency). The money shot.
- Click into the app, Cost door. Paste the SMS: `You qualify for 1000, repay 1150 in 30 days`.
- Click "Read my offer" → the confirm step shows the parsed fields → confirm.
- Land on the result. Hold on the **182.5% APR** and the **value-vs-pay bar**.
- Voiceover: "The dark bar is what you received. The orange is what the credit costs on
  top. And the math is shown, computed in code, never guessed by AI."
- Open "Show the math" for one second to prove it.
- FALLBACK: if AI parse is throttled, use the "Pick a loan" dropdown (M-KOPA / Watu)
  instead of the paste. Same result card.

### 0:45–1:05 — Check a lender (Accountability)
- Cost result already shows the licence line. Then go to the Lender door, type `Mogo`.
- Show the status glyph + "Regulated under a different regime" (not falsely "unlicensed"),
  the **Official finding** badge (CAK) vs the **Reported concern** (press), and the
  **"Check the live register" link with the as-of date**.
- Voiceover: "A public fact, sourced and dated. We separate an official regulator finding
  from a news report, and link you to the live register to confirm today."
- Then type `QuickCash` to show a clean "NOT on the register" with the warning glyph.

### 1:05–1:35 — Take action + privacy (Safety, Reporting & Protection)
- In the action box type: "They are calling everyone in my phonebook and threatening to
  take my boda. My name is John Mwangi, 0712345678."
- Click "Show me what to do". FIRST point at the **privacy banner**: "Private by default,
  removed before sending: phone number, name." 
- Voiceover: "Your details are stripped on your device before anything is sent, and
  nothing is stored."
- Then show: the law (Data Protection Act), **both bodies** (ODPC + CBK), the evidence
  checklist, and the **prepared complaint with a case reference**.

### 1:35–1:50 — Reporting leads to protection
- Click "Draft a stop-contact letter". Show the cease-and-desist letter to the lender.
- Voiceover: "The complaint to the regulator is slow. This letter goes straight to the
  lender and demands the harassment stop now."

### 1:50–2:05 — Helper workspace (Stability & Social Cohesion)
- Click "Helper? Manage several people's cases" in the header.
- Add a case with initials "J.M." and a short note. Show the list, open the case (it
  drops into the action box).
- Voiceover: "A paralegal or chief helping a whole community keeps each case here, on
  their device only. One tool, many people."

### 2:05–2:25 — One answer, three channels + languages (scalability + inclusion)
- Back on the landing page, scroll to the three-channel section: web, WhatsApp, USSD,
  same answer.
- Voiceover: "The same civic engine on a smartphone, over WhatsApp, or on a basic
  feature phone by USSD."
- Toggle the language to Kiswahili, then Sheng, to show the whole interface translate.
- Toggle the country selector to South Africa: the regulator becomes the NCR, the law
  becomes POPIA and the National Credit Act, the currency becomes rand.
- Voiceover: "Adding a country is adding a data pack, not a rewrite. Kenya today, South
  Africa already running."

### 2:25–2:40 — Close (why it matters + AI build)
- Return to the true-cost card.
- Voiceover: "Deni turns a right and a public fact into something an ordinary person can
  act on. Built spec-first with AI coding tools, with a hard rule: AI never states the
  law or a number. The law comes from sourced data, the money from tested code."
- End card: "Deni. Know your rights as a borrower. OSF × Andela."

## Honesty lines to keep true on camera

- Never call a named lender a "scam." State the sourced fact ("not on the register as of
  <date>") or a regulator/court finding.
- Say "not legal or financial advice" once, and let the on-screen disclaimer carry it.
- Label the WhatsApp and USSD channels as running on provider sandboxes.
- Say the South Africa pack is a demonstration pack with representative figures.
